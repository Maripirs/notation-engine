notation-engine MusicXML engraving, canvas rendering and playback in the browser

A from-scratch music notation engine: it imports and exports MusicXML, engraves it onto a canvas, plays it back, and lets you edit it. Edits are region-scoped, so changing one note lays out and repaints only its measure, not the whole score.

GitHub npm Quickstart

Try it

  • Click a note, then press ↑ / ↓ to transpose it. Only that measure repaints; the timing readout below the score compares it with a full repaint.
  • Undo and redo with Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z, or the buttons.
  • Zoom in and out: the whole score re-engraves at the new size and reflows onto new lines.
  • Press Play in the Playback section: the score scrolls to follow a moving cursor.
  • Open your own MusicXML file (for example one exported from MuseScore), edit it, then Save to download it.
